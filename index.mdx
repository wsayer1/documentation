Gathere is a simple platform for **hosting and discovering free events** — startup meetups, talks, workshops, and meet-and-greets. Anyone with an account can host their own events and register for others, so the same person can be both an organizer and a guest.

## Good to know

- **Events are always free.** There are no tickets to pay for and no payment setup.
- **Public or private.** Public events appear on the Explore page for everyone; private events are reachable only by sharing their link.
- **In-person or online.** Online events keep the meeting link hidden until a guest registers.
- **No email notifications yet.** You keep track of your events and tickets inside Gathere.

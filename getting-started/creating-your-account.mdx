You need a free account to register for events or host your own. Creating one takes a few seconds.

## Sign up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-up form">
    From the landing page, choose **Sign up**. A small window opens over the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Type your **full name**, **email address**, and a **password** (at least 6 characters). Your name is what other members and your guests will see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Select **Create account**. You're signed in right away and taken to the Explore page — no email confirmation step to wait for.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sign in

Already have an account? Choose **Sign in** instead, enter your email and password, and you'll land back on Explore.

<Tip>
  If you see "Incorrect email or password," double-check both fields. If you see "An account with this email already exists," switch to **Sign in** rather than signing up again.
</Tip>

## Staying signed in

Gathere keeps you signed in between visits, so you won't need to log in every time. When you want to leave, use **Sign out** from the menu.

## What happens behind the scenes

The moment you sign up, Gathere creates a **member profile** for you using your name. You can add a photo, bio, and more later — see [Your profile](/profiles/your-profile).

Once you sign in, Gathere shows a simple sidebar with everything you need. Here's what each area is for.

## The main areas

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Explore" icon="compass" href="/attending/discovering-events">
    The home screen after you sign in. Browse all public, upcoming events and search or filter them.
  </Card>

  <Card title="My events" icon="calendar-days" href="/hosting/managing-an-event">
    Everything you're hosting — upcoming events, drafts you haven't published, and past events.
  </Card>

  <Card title="My tickets" icon="ticket" href="/attending/managing-your-tickets">
    Every event you've registered for or joined the waitlist on.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Saved" icon="bookmark" href="/attending/saving-events">
    Events you've bookmarked to look at again later.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Profile" icon="user" href="/profiles/your-profile">
    Your member profile — photo, bio, interests, links, and the events you host.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Where you start

Signed-in members land on **Explore** by default. Selecting the Gathere logo always takes you back to the landing page.

## Creating an event from anywhere

The **Create event** button lives on your My events screen. Wherever you are, you can head there to start a new event — see [Creating an event](/hosting/creating-an-event).

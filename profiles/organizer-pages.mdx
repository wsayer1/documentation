On Gathere, your profile is also your **organizer page**. When someone looks at your profile, they see who you are _and_ the upcoming public events you're hosting — all in one place.

## Viewing another member's profile

Anywhere a host's name appears — for example on an event page under "Hosted by" — you can select it to open that member's profile. Any signed-in member can view any other member's profile this way.

## What an organizer page shows

- The member's photo, bio, role, company, location, interests, and links.
- Their **upcoming public events**, so you can discover more of what they're putting on.
- Stats: how many events they've hosted and how many are coming up.

<Note>
  Only **public** events show on an organizer page. Private events stay hidden and are reachable only through their shared link.
</Note>

## Making the most of it

If you host regularly, a complete profile makes your organizer page more inviting. Add a photo, a short bio, and your links so guests know who's behind the event — see [Your profile](/profiles/your-profile).

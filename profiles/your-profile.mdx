Every account comes with a member profile. A profile is created automatically when you sign up, using your name — you can fill in the rest whenever you like.

## What's on a profile

- **Photo** — a picture of you (up to 2 MB, images only).
- **Name** — required; this is what guests and other members see.
- **Bio** — a short "about me".
- **Job title** and **company**.
- **Location** — your city or region.
- **Interests** — topic tags like AI, Web, or Product.
- **Links** — your website, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, and GitHub.

Everything except your name is optional.

## Editing your profile

Open **Profile** in the sidebar, then choose to edit it. Update any field and save. Your photo uploads securely, and only you can change your own profile.

## Who can see it

Profiles are visible to **signed-in members only**. Signed-out visitors can't see them — on a public event page viewed while signed out, the host simply shows as "the organizer".

<Note>
  Your profile also shows stats: the number of events you've hosted and your upcoming events. Your "attending" count is shown only to you, because your registrations are private.
</Note>

Every event you host has a **manage screen**. Open it from **My events** by selecting the event, or from the event's own page with **Manage event**.

## At a glance

The top of the screen shows three live counts:

- **Registered** — guests with a confirmed spot.
- **Waitlisted** — guests waiting for a spot to open.
- **Checked in** — confirmed guests you've marked as arrived.

## Things you can do here

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="View page" icon="eye">
    Open the public event page exactly as guests see it.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Edit" icon="pen">
    Change any detail — title, time, venue or meeting link, capacity, visibility. Editing a published event keeps it published.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Copy link" icon="link">
    Grab the shareable event link to invite guests.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Guest list" icon="users">
    See everyone who's registered or waitlisted, and check them in.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The guest list

Below the counts is your full guest list, in the order people registered. Each entry shows the guest's name and whether they're registered or waitlisted. You can **copy the guest list** to your clipboard — handy for a printout or a spreadsheet.

Every event is either **public** or **private**. You choose when you create it, and you can change it later by editing the event.

## Public events

Public events are listed on the **Explore** page, where any member can find them, search for them, and register. Choose public when you want the widest possible audience — an open meetup, a community talk, a workshop anyone can join.

## Private events

Private events **never appear on Explore**. The only way to reach one is through its link. Anyone you share the link with can open the event and register, but people who don't have the link won't stumble across it.

Choose private for invite-only gatherings — a team dinner, a small founder circle, a closed beta session.

## Sharing an event link

On the event page or the manage screen, use **Copy link** (or **Copy event link**) to grab the shareable address, then send it wherever you like — chat, email, or a message.

<Note>
  Visibility only controls whether an event is _listed_. A private event's link still works for anyone who has it, so share it thoughtfully.
</Note>

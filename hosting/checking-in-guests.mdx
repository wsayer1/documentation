On the day of your event, the guest list on the [manage screen](/hosting/managing-an-event) doubles as your check-in sheet.

## Check someone in

Next to each confirmed guest is a **Check in** button. Select it when the guest arrives and it switches to **Checked in**. Changed by mistake? Select it again to undo.

The **Checked in** count at the top updates as you go, so you can see attendance at a glance.

## A few things to know

- Only **confirmed** guests can be checked in. Waitlisted guests need a spot to open up first.
- Check-in is just for your own tracking — guests aren't notified when you check them in.
- You can check people in from a phone or laptop; the list is the same everywhere you sign in.

<Tip>
  Before the doors open, use **Copy guest list** on the manage screen to keep a backup of who's expected.
</Tip>

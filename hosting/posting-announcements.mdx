Announcements let you send short updates to everyone registered for your event — an agenda, a room change, a parking note, or a thank-you afterward.

## Post an update

On the [manage screen](/hosting/managing-an-event), find the **Announcements** section. Type your message and choose **Post update**. It's saved right away.

## Who sees it

Your announcements appear on the event page under **Updates from the host**, visible to:

- **You**, the host.
- Any guest who has **registered** (including waitlisted guests).

Guests who haven't registered don't see them.

<Note>
  Gathere doesn't send emails, so announcements show up on the event page rather than in an inbox. Let guests know to check the event page for updates.
</Note>

## Removing an update

Each announcement has a delete button. Remove one and it disappears for everyone. You can post as many updates as you like over the life of the event.

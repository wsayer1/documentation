If plans fall through, you can cancel an event from its [manage screen](/hosting/managing-an-event).

## How to cancel

At the bottom of the manage screen, choose **Cancel event**. You'll be asked to confirm so it doesn't happen by accident. Confirm and the event is marked cancelled.

## What happens next

- The event shows a **Cancelled** label everywhere it appears.
- **Registration closes** — no one new can sign up.
- Guests see the cancelled status on the event page and in their [tickets](/attending/managing-your-tickets), under _Past & cancelled_.
- The event drops off the Explore page.

<Warning>
  Cancelling can't be undone from the app. If there's any chance the event will still happen, consider editing the details or posting an [announcement](/hosting/posting-announcements) instead.
</Warning>

## Cancel vs. delete

Cancelling keeps a record of the event and lets your guests see that it was called off. There's no separate delete step — cancelling is the way to take an event down.

Some events have a limited number of spots. When every spot is taken, Gathere doesn't turn you away — it adds you to a **waitlist**.

## Joining the waitlist

If an event is full when you go to register, the button reads **Join waitlist**. Select it and your status becomes **You're on the waitlist**. You keep your place in line based on when you joined.

## How a spot opens up

When someone with a confirmed spot cancels, the person who has been waiting longest is **moved up to a confirmed spot automatically**. There's nothing you need to do — if you're next in line, your status changes to going on its own.

<Note>
  Because Gathere doesn't send emails yet, check [My tickets](/attending/managing-your-tickets) or the event page to see whether your waitlisted spot has become confirmed.
</Note>

## Leaving the waitlist

You can step off the waitlist at any time by choosing **Cancel registration** on the event page.

## Events with no limit

Many events have no capacity limit at all. For those, everyone who registers is confirmed right away and there's never a waitlist.

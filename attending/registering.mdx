Registering saves your spot at an event. It's free, and it takes one click.

## Register

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the event">
    Select an event from Explore, a shared link, or a host's organizer page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Register">
    In the panel on the right, select **Register**. If you're not signed in yet, you'll be asked to sign in first, then your spot is reserved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're going">
    The panel updates to **You're going**. That's it — your registration also shows up under [My tickets](/attending/managing-your-tickets).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Online events and the meeting link

For online events, the meeting link stays hidden until you register. Once you're confirmed as going, the link appears right on the event page, ready to open when it's time.

<Note>
  If an event is full when you register, you'll join the waitlist instead of getting a confirmed spot. See [Waitlists](/attending/waitlists).
</Note>

## Cancelling

Changed your mind? Open the event and choose **Cancel registration**. Your spot is released — and if the event was full, the first person on the waitlist is moved up automatically.

## When you can't register

Registration closes when an event has ended or has been cancelled by the host. In those cases you'll see a note instead of the Register button.

Not ready to register, but don't want to lose track of an event? **Save** it. Saving is a private bookmark — only you can see what you've saved.

## Save an event

Look for the **bookmark icon** on an event card or the **Save event** button on an event page. Select it and the event is added to your Saved list. Select it again to remove the bookmark.

## Find your saved events

Open **Saved** in the sidebar to see everything you've bookmarked. From there you can open any event to register, add it to your calendar, or share it.

<Note>
  Saving an event is not the same as registering for it. Saving just bookmarks it for later — to reserve a spot you still need to [register](/attending/registering).
</Note>

## Who can see your saves

Your saved events are completely private. Hosts and other members can't see what you've bookmarked.

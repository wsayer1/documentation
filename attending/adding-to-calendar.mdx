So an event doesn't slip your mind, you can add it straight to your own calendar from its event page.

## Where to find it

On any **upcoming, published** event page, look for the **Add to calendar** option in the panel on the right. You'll see two choices:

- **Google Calendar** — opens Google Calendar with the event details already filled in. Just confirm to save it.
- **Download (.ics)** — downloads a standard calendar file you can open in Apple Calendar, Outlook, or most other calendar apps.

Either way, the event's title, date, time, and location come along automatically.

<Note>
  Add to calendar appears only for upcoming events that are open. It isn't shown to the event's host, or for events that have already happened or been cancelled.
</Note>

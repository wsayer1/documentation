**My tickets** collects every event you've registered for or joined the waitlist on, so you always know what's coming up.

## What you'll see

Your tickets are split into two lists:

- **Upcoming** — events still to come that you're going to or waitlisted for.
- **Past & cancelled** — events that have already happened, plus any the host later cancelled.

Each row shows the event, its date and time, and a badge telling you your status:

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Going** | You have a confirmed spot. |
| **Waitlisted** | You're in line for a spot if one opens up. |
| **Cancelled** | The host cancelled this event. |

## Opening an event

Select any ticket to jump straight to that event's page — handy for grabbing the meeting link, reading updates from the host, or cancelling.

## Cancelling a registration

To give up a spot, open the event and choose **Cancel registration**. It disappears from your upcoming tickets, and if the event was full, the next waitlisted guest moves up.
